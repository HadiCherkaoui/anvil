Upstream Maven repository metadata

The files covered by this reference are verbatim captures of Maven repository
metadata published publicly by the MinecraftForge and NeoForged projects. They
are used unmodified as test fixtures.

They are machine-generated indexes of released version strings. They contain no
creative expression authored by this project, and no copyright is claimed over
them here. They are reproduced solely to pin the parser's behaviour against real
upstream responses.

Sources:
  https://maven.minecraftforge.net/net/minecraftforge/forge/maven-metadata.xml
  https://maven.neoforged.net/releases/net/neoforged/neoforge/maven-metadata.xml
